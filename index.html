First dibs on
the good stuff.

Finally, it’s okay to be an Early Bird. We’re working on a cool feature to make buying and selling easier: for a small reservation fee, sellers hold selected items for Early Bird+ members for the first 15 minutes of their yard sale.

Sellers get the price they want for that vintage video game console or velvet Elvis painting, and Early Birds get first dibs.

The final sale price is between the seller and the shopper. We’re just the matchmaker. The hold fee simply helps keep the app free for everyone.

A few good questions

Before you head out.

Is Early Bird free?

Yes. Browsing the map and listing a sale are free. An Early Bird+ tier will offer higher-end tools for serious Early Birds who want a little more.

Are you in every state?

Nope. We’re starting in Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach counties. This is home for us, and it’s also one of the most concentrated yard sale markets in the country. So if we can make it here, the other states will follow soon enough.

Do I need an account?

You don’t need an account to browse the public map or to list a sale, but you will need to confirm an email we send you to verify you’re human.

Is my data safe?

Your email signs you in. Photo text-reading and visit history stay under your control, and we do not sell your information. The Privacy Policy lists everything we collect and how long we keep it.

Will the app be on Android phones?

Probably. We are a small team and are focusing on iPhone users for now. But it’s on our to-do list.
