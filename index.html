A few good questions

Before you head out.

Is Early Bird free?

Yes. Browsing the map and listing a sale are free. An Early Bird+ tier will offer higher-end features for serious Early Birds who want a little more.

Where can I find sales?

We’re starting in Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach counties. This is home for us, and we want to build a useful local community before expanding.

Do I need an account?

You don’t need an account to browse the public map. To list your own sale, you confirm your email through a link we send you.

Are all listings posted by sellers?

No. Listings can come from sellers, municipal permit filings, other public sale information, or community submissions. We review aggregated and community-submitted listings before publication.

Will the app be on Android phones?

Not for now. We are a small team and we need time to get each user platform right. But we will soon, we promise.
