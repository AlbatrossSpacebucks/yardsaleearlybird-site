A few good questions

Before you head out.

Is Early Bird free?

Yes. Browsing the map and listing a sale are free. Use it, enjoy it, share it with your friends.

Will it stay free?

Access to the map and lists will remain free. However, as we scale and grow our advanced tools, we will have to start charging a few bucks per month to cover server costs. When that time comes, we’ll send you a notification. Early adopters will naturally receive the OG discounted rate—and a badge.

Are you in every state?

We’re starting in Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach counties. This is home for us, and it’s also one of the most concentrated yard sale markets in the country. So if we can make it here, we can make it anywhere.

Do I need an account?

Not to browse the map. To list a sale or use advanced tools, you must sign in with your email.

Is my data safe?

Yes. We only collect what we need. Your email signs you in. Photo text-reading and visit history stay under your control. We don’t track and we don’t sell your information. The Privacy Policy explains it all in detail.

Will the app be on Android phones?

Probably. As with all startups, it depends on demand, but we’d love to be.
